## About this project

An HR dashboard linking satisfaction, work-life balance, salary hike, marital status, overtime, tenure and job role to attrition, with tenure, income bands and satisfaction levels grouped for analysis.

## Key findings

- Employees working overtime were the most likely to leave.
- Poor work-life balance correlated strongly with attrition.
- A high salary did not guarantee retention; satisfaction played a stronger role.
