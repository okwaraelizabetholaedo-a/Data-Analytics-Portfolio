# Breast cancer outcomes analysis

**Tools:** Power BI, DAX, Power Query

*What patterns exist between patient characteristics, cancer progression and recorded outcomes?*

|---|---|---|---|
| patient records | recorded survival, 3,408 alive and 616 deceased | recorded mortality, Stage IIA to IIIC | recorded mortality, ER-positive vs ER-negative |

## About this project

Three interactive dashboards covering demographics, clinical characteristics, stage, tumour size and grade, lymph-node involvement, hormone receptor status and survival.

## Key findings

- Recorded mortality also rose with lymph-node involvement, from 9.9% (1–3 positive nodes) to 37.5% (10 or more).
- Differences appeared across age, race and marital-status groups, which raises questions about access, diagnosis and outcomes.

> Patterns within this dataset, not proof of causation or population-level cancer risk.
