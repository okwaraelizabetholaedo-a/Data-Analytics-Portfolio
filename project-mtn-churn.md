## About this project

Two dashboard pages covering revenue by gender and subscription plan, purchases by MTN device, churn by state, tenure by month and data usage by churn status.

## Key findings

- With no direct speed column, I built a speed proxy from the available data and tested the logic.
- Defining who truly churned took careful filtering and repeated DAX measure testing.
