About the project

The project uses three interactive dashboards to examine demographic and clinical characteristics alongside cancer progression and recorded outcomes. The analysis covers tumour size and grade, cancer stage, lymph-node involvement, hormone receptor status and survival.

Key Findings

Recorded mortality increased with lymph-node involvement, rising from 9.9% among patients with 1–3 positive nodes to 37.5% among patients with 10 or more positive nodes.
Differences appeared across age, race and marital-status groups, highlighting areas that may warrant deeper investigation into access, diagnosis and outcomes.
These findings describe patterns within this dataset and should not be interpreted as proof of causation or as population-level cancer risk.
