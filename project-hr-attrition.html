01 / Overview

About the project

This project analyses employee data to identify patterns in workforce attrition and understand the characteristics of employees who leave an organisation. The analysis considers demographics, job roles, compensation, job satisfaction, overtime, tenure and other workforce factors.

03 / Insights

Key Findings

Attrition patterns vary across employee groups, highlighting differences in workforce retention across roles, departments and demographic segments.
Job satisfaction, overtime, compensation and career characteristics provide useful dimensions for exploring differences between employees who remain and those who leave.
Interactive workforce analysis makes it possible to identify employee segments that may require deeper investigation from an HR and retention perspective.
The findings describe patterns within the analysed dataset and should not be interpreted as proof that any individual factor directly causes employee attrition.
